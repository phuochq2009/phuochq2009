## Research Experience

### Vietnamese Speaker Verification Under Noisy Conditions
**Co-Researcher · Paper accepted to ICIIT 2027 · May – Sep 2026**

*Do Teacher-Student Alignment and Gaussian KL Improve Short and Noisy Vietnamese Speaker Verification?*

- Evaluated knowledge distillation and supervised adaptation strategies for ECAPA-TDNN across **27 noisy Vietnamese conditions**.
- Investigated teacher-student vector alignment and Gaussian KL regularization.
- Found that the simpler AAM-Softmax baseline outperformed the more complex alignment approaches, achieving **12.7% EER on clean audio** and **23.8% EER at 0 dB**.
